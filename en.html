Our Rooms

We offer both private rooms and dormitory beds. Shared facilities on every floor — toilets, sinks, free Wi-Fi throughout. No curfew.

Twin Room

5 of our 6 twin rooms have 2 futons; 1 room has 2 single beds. Side table, wardrobe, hairdryer and charging outlets in every room. Individual AC per room. Large windows and lockable door — great for friends or family wanting privacy.

Dormitory 2A

2 bunk beds with personal light, outlet, curtain & fan per bed. Valuables locker, AC and large window. Cozy and bright atmosphere.

Dormitory 2B

2 bunk beds with personal amenities plus wardrobe for clothes and luggage. Easy-access stairs to upper bunk. AC included. Very comfortable stay.

FAQ

What are the check-in and check-out times? ›

Check-in is 15:00–24:00 (self check-in with door code). Check-out is by 11:00 the following day.

Can I store luggage before check-in? ›

Yes! Free on your check-in and check-out day.

Do rooms have showers and toilets? ›

Shared toilets and washrooms are on every floor. Shower rooms include free shampoo, conditioner and body soap. Toothbrushes and bath towels available for rent at the front desk.

Is the coin laundry available anytime? ›

Available 24 hours. 2 washing machines and 2 dryers on the 1st floor. Detergent is free.

Is there parking? ›

No dedicated parking, but there is a coin parking lot 1 minute walk away (¥550/24 hrs). Mitsui Repark Nagoya Omiyacho 3-chome, capacity 20 cars.

Is there a curfew? ›

No curfew. Simply use the door password to enter at any time.

Are there any house rules? ›

Quiet hours are 22:00–07:00. Parties and events are not allowed.

What is the smoking policy? ›

The building is non-smoking. Smoking is allowed only in the rooftop smoking area. Out of consideration for our neighbours, please do not smoke at the entrance or around the building.
